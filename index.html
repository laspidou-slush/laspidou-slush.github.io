01 /THE VISIT

A UNIVERSITY
READY TO CONNECT.

This is a focused introduction to the University of Thessaly's research-based entrepreneurship ecosystem, shared by Vice-Rector Professor Chrysi Laspidou during Slush 2026.

Through One Planet Thessaly, the University's Innovation and Technology Transfer Unit & Entrepreneurship Center, research teams can explore knowledge transfer, intellectual property and engagement with industry. At Slush, the priority is straightforward: meaningful introductions and follow-up conversations.

ONE EVENT. ONE POINT OF CONTACT. MULTIPLE PATHS TO COLLABORATE.
